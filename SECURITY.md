# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue. Report it privately through GitHub: **Security → Report a vulnerability** in this repository. We confirm receipt within three working days.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it and what an attacker could do with it.

## How the app is protected

The controls are described in the [target architecture](docs/target-architecture.md), sections 6 (identity and authorization), 8 (security architecture) and 9 (logging and audit). Known gaps and planned hardening are listed in [`docs/todo-later.md`](docs/todo-later.md).
